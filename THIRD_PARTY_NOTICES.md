# Third-party code and assets

The root MIT license covers original Spectral source code only. Third-party code, SDKs, packages, fonts, artwork and textures retain their own licenses and notices.

- `third_party/stb_image.h`: license choices are reproduced in the file.
- `include/glad`, `src/glad.c`, and `include/KHR`: retain their embedded notices.
- GLFW is obtained separately through CMake; its upstream license applies.
- `spectacles/OpticsLab`: derived from the Snapchat Spectacles sample identified in `PROVENANCE.json`. See `SNAP_SAMPLE_LICENSE.md` for sample licensing and linked platform terms. Bundled `.lspkg` packages retain their own terms and notices; the root MIT license does not relicense them.
- HDRI and concrete texture assets: redistribution provenance has not yet been verified. This repository is being prepared privately; review or replace these assets before a public release.

Public-release review is separate from the secret scan. No blanket MIT claim is made for third-party assets.
